Target Audience Personas

Primary visitor archetypes shaping navigation structure and content priority.

Automotive Buyer
Age 25 to 55 : Showroom Visitor
  • Intent: Browse brand dealerships, compare servicing hours, and locate service bays.
  • Friction: Hidden showroom phone numbers and outdated operating hours.
  • Design Response: Prominent contact cards, clear opening hours, and one-tap directions.
Dining & Night Patron
Age 18 to 45 : Weekend Visitor
  • Intent: Discover cafes, family restaurants, late-night dining, and parking availability.
  • Friction: Cluttered menus and difficulty locating venues within the complex.
  • Design Response: Visual restaurant cards with category pills and parking wayfinding.
Commercial Event Planner
Age 30 to 60 : Business Organizer
  • Intent: Evaluate carnival grounds, event staging capacities, and leasing terms.
  • Friction: Buried inquiry forms and lack of venue dimension specifications.
  • Design Response: Dedicated commercial space overview with direct leasing inquiry triggers.
