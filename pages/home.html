Family, friends, beyond.

From tranquil riverfront glamping retreats and dynamic non-powered family parks to premier convention venues and executive co-working suites—uncover the multifaceted destinations shaping Auto-City.

01

Nadi Glamping Suites

Penang's premier riverfront eco-glamping sanctuary nestled within Auto-City Riverpark. Unwind in air-conditioned timber cabins or romantic safari tents equipped with luxury King Koil beds, private en-suites, and starlit evening campfires along the tranquil riverbank.

Explore Glamping Suites
02

Lavenda Fun Park

Penang's first non-powered outdoor adventure park spanning expansive green landscapes at Auto-City Phase 2. Experience zero-electricity kinetic thrills with massive multi-lane rainbow hill slides, sculptural lavender climbing towers, sky rope bridges, and joyful active family play.

Discover Lavenda Park
03

Event space rental & team building

Over 10,000 sq. ft. of versatile multi-purpose indoor and semi-outdoor event halls (Concept Hall & Sky TV Stage) accommodating up to 800 guests. Complete your corporate retreat with adrenaline-fueled group bonding, from Wild Kart off-road racing to RaceRoom simulators and tournament pickleball.

Hubbiz - Commercial Office Space
